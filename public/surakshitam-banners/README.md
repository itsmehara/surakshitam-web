## Responsive use

- Desktop ratio: 16:9, 1536 × 864.
- Keep live copy in the left content column.
- For narrow/mobile views, use `object-position: center right` or `background-position: center right`.
- For best mobile readability, stack the live copy above the image instead of overlaying it over the product group.
- Do not crop from the far left; the subject and product labels are deliberately positioned in the central-right safe zone.

## Source-reference policy

- The real supplied product photographs were authoritative for container shape, cap, product color, label and attached soap stickers.
- Old poster backgrounds, large surrounding logos, claims, icons, badges and promotional text were excluded.
- Products with placeholder-only source images were not invented. This includes Washing Machine Liquid, Herbal Hair Pack, Hair Oil, Hair Serum, Rosemary Hair Spray and several newer skin-care entries.

## Generation method

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using one tailored prompt per banner and the relevant source product photographs. The selected Nimba Home concept was used only for general mood, lighting, botanical palette and negative-space logic—not for its brand, logo, copy or UI.
